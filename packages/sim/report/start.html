The loop

Six stations, once a day at 08:00 or whenever you ask. The circle stays open at the last station on purpose: nothing reaches the app you use until you merge it.

    The population

    Who uses Beobeo

    Each persona has fixed habits (weekly buys, dip rules, harvesting) and a mind that decides everything else in its own voice. Curiosity is the chance they wander the app at random on a given day. What they said each day lives in the daily reports.

    Choosing the next change

    How we decide what to fix

    Every cycle works on your own top open request first (they live in REQUESTS.md, in your words, each with a checklist of what "done" means). After that, persona feedback decides. Every complaint, wish and compliment is tagged with a theme, and themes are scored:

    Breadth weighs most, so one loud persona can't outrank a quiet majority. Praise never scores: it's a list of things not to break. When a complaint turns out to be caused by the simulator itself (a persona misreading its own notes, say), it's set aside and never becomes product work.
